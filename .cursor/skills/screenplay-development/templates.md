# Screenplay Development Templates

Use only the template required by the active phase. Replace brackets with project-specific material; do not leave placeholders in a final deliverable.

## 1. Development brief

```markdown
# [Working title]

## Idea
[A specific person, in a specific situation, must do a specific thing.]

## Why now
[The event that makes continued normal life impossible.]

## Why cinema
[The recurring actions, places, sounds, and images that carry the story.]

## Audience promise
[The experience the movie must deliver.]

## Primary uncertainty
[The one unresolved decision that most changes the movie.]
```

## 11. Dialogue beat map

```markdown
| Beat | Speaker action | Listener response | Failed expectation | New tactic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [directed verb] | [response] | [what failed] | [new directed verb] |
```

After mapping, remove the table from the screenplay and write playable dialogue.

Dialogue pass:

- Cut greeting and throat-clearing.
- Cut repeated image/action information.
- Replace stated emotion with behavior where possible.
- Replace explanation with tactical use.
- Check the unsaid meaning.
- Check voice against the character's life.
- Read aloud and compress.
